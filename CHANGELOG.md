## 0.1.0

*The first version. Needs macOS 14 or later and Strom Research 1.21 or later on the same Mac.*

- **Strom research on the Mac desktop.** A widget in four sizes (small to extra large) shows what the research is
  working on now, what it found in the last 24 hours (people, families, sources, solved conflicts) and what waits for
  a decision. One research per widget; with more of them, each widget picks its own.
- **Names can be hidden.** The widget can show the overview without names of people, for a desktop others see.
- **A click opens the Strom app** at the matching place.
- **The Strom Widget app** lists the research on this Mac, shows how to add the widget, sets the language
  (Czech, English, German; by default the research's language) and uninstalls everything with one button.
- **The data stays on the Mac.** The widget reads only the files the research writes on the same Mac; nothing goes
  over the network.
